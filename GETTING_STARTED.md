## Your First Field Visit

### Before You Go

**Print/Download**:
- [ ] `02_Problem_Research/INTERVIEW_TEMPLATE.md`
- [ ] `02_Problem_Research/FIELD_OBSERVATION_TEMPLATE.md`

**Bring**:
- [ ] Camera or smartphone for photos
- [ ] Measuring tape
- [ ] Notebook
- [ ] Printed templates

### During the Visit

1. **Observe first** - Fill out Field Observation Template
2. **Take photos** - Lots of them! (See photo checklist in template)
3. **Interview stakeholders** - Use Interview Template
4. **Be respectful** - Ask permission, explain your mission

### After the Visit

1. **Transfer photos** to `08_Assets/Photos/Workshop_Photos/`
2. **Name photos properly**: `YYYYMMDD_Location_Subject_###.jpg`
3. **Complete templates** if you didn't finish on-site
4. **Save templates** in `02_Problem_Research/Interviews/` and `/Observations/`
5. **Update** your weekly checklist with learnings

## Your First Brainstorming Session

### Preparation (Do This First)

1. Read `03_Solution_Ideation/IDEATION_FRAMEWORK.md`
2. Set aside 2-3 hours of uninterrupted time
3. Gather: Paper, pens, post-it notes

### During Brainstorming

1. **Don't judge** - All ideas are valid initially
2. **Go for quantity** - Aim for 20+ concepts minimum
3. **Use the prompts** in the Ideation Framework
4. **Sketch** your ideas (doesn't have to be perfect)

### After Brainstorming

1. **Document all ideas** in `03_Solution_Ideation/Ideas/`
2. **Take photos** of sketches and put in `08_Assets/Diagrams/`
3. **Schedule evaluation** session (read Evaluation Framework)

## Essential Habits for Success

### 1. Document Everything

If you did something, write it down. Future you will thank present you.

### 2. Take Photos

Photos are invaluable. Take more than you think you need. You can always delete later.

### 3. Back Up Regularly

Use Google Drive, Dropbox, or similar. Backup at least weekly.

### 4. Stay User-Centered

Every decision should consider: "How does this help workshop owners and workers?"

### 5. Iterate Quickly

Don't aim for perfection. Build, test, learn, improve.
