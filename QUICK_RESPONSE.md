Hey! Great questions. Here's how we're thinking:

**1. Next Steps:**
- This week: Pull together what the team already knows, plan workshop visits
- Weeks 2-4: Visit 5-10 workshops, document everything, analyze what we find
- Weeks 5-12: Build 2-3 prototypes, test them in real workshops
- After that: Refine based on feedback, then scale to more workshops

Basically: research → brainstorm → build → test → improve → scale

**2. Solution Nature:**
We're thinking **hybrid approach**:
- **Mainly technical** (70%) - A physical device/system to filter/remove lint from air. Probably something like air filtration or improved ventilation. Must be cheap (<₹10k), low power, simple to maintain.
- **Some behavioral** (20%) - Simple maintenance routines and usage guidelines so people actually use it properly
- **Some social** (10%) - Community adoption through word-of-mouth, peer learning, maybe group buying

Why hybrid? The technical part solves the actual problem, but it only works if people use it right (behavioral) and if others trust it enough to adopt it (social).

**3. Pictorial Evidence:**
- Get permission first from workshop owners (always ask before photographing)
- Use phone cameras - everyone has one, good enough quality
- Follow a checklist: workshop overview, lint on machines/ceiling/walls, ventilation points, workers (with permission), existing solutions they've tried
- Store everything organized in our GitHub repo with proper naming
- Respect privacy - blur faces if requested, don't share without permission
- Goal: 50-100 photos from 5-10 workshops in first 2 weeks

The photos will help us: show the problem clearly, understand why existing solutions don't work, and later show the impact of our solution.
