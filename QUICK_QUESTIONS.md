# Quick Questions for Your Team

Use these questions to get information from your team. Pick the ones that are most relevant.

---

## 📊 About Workshop Observations

**If they've visited workshops:**

1. Which workshops did you visit? (Names/locations)
2. How many looms did each have?
3. What did you notice about the lint problem?
4. What specific issues did workshop owners mention?
5. Do you have photos? (Even phone pics are helpful)
6. What surprised you most?

---

## 🔍 About Existing Solutions They Found

**If they've researched solutions:**

1. What solutions did you find? (List them)
2. How much do they cost?
3. Why aren't workshops using them already?
4. What's the biggest barrier? (Price? Complexity? Availability?)
5. Did you see anything clever or interesting?
6. Any DIY solutions people are trying?

---

## 💡 About Their Ideas

**To get them thinking creatively:**

1. If money was no object, what would you do?
2. If you had to solve this with only local materials, what would you try?
3. Have you seen similar problems solved elsewhere? (In other industries, other countries?)
4. What's the simplest thing we could try first?
5. What would workers actually use every day?

---

## 🎯 Quick Assessment Questions

**To understand their perspective:**

1. On a scale of 1-10, how serious is this problem?
2. What would "success" look like to you?
3. What's the biggest challenge we'll face?
4. Who should we talk to for more information?
5. What do you think we should focus on first?

---

## 📸 Assets to Collect

**Ask them to share if they have:**

- [ ] Photos of workshops
- [ ] Photos of lint buildup
- [ ] Photos of existing solutions they saw
- [ ] Contact info for workshop owners willing to help
- [ ] Links to products/solutions they researched
- [ ] Rough sketches of ideas (even napkin drawings!)
- [ ] Cost estimates they found

---

## 🗣️ Sample Conversation Starters

**For a casual chat:**

*"Hey, you mentioned you looked into the lint problem. What did you find out?"*

*"What were your main takeaways from the workshops you visited?"*

*"If you had to pitch one solution right now, what would it be?"*

*"What do you think is the biggest reason workshops aren't fixing this themselves?"*

*"Who else should we be talking to about this?"*

---

## ⚡ The 3 Most Important Questions

If you only ask 3 things, ask these:

1. **What existing solutions did you find, and why aren't they working?**
   (This prevents us from reinventing the wheel)

2. **What do workshop owners actually want/need?**
   (This keeps us user-centered)

3. **What's your best idea so far?**
   (This starts the creative process)

---

*Use these questions to extract valuable information your team already has!*
