### Daily Maintenance (1 minute)

**Quick Check**:
- [ ] Device is running normally
- [ ] No unusual noises or smells
- [ ] Air flowing from outlet

---

### Weekly Maintenance (5 minutes)

**Filter Check**:

1. Turn OFF power
2. Open filter access door
3. Look at filter - is it very dirty?
4. If very dirty, proceed to monthly cleaning
5. If acceptable, close door and resume operation

---

### Monthly Maintenance (15-20 minutes)

**Filter Cleaning**:

1. ⚠️ Turn OFF and unplug device
2. Open filter access door
3. Remove filter carefully
4. Take filter outside
5. Gently tap to remove loose lint
6. Use soft brush to clean filter surface
7. If washable: Rinse with clean water, let dry completely (24 hours)
8. Reinstall dry filter
9. Close access door
10. Restore power and turn on

**General Cleaning**:
- Wipe exterior with damp cloth
- Clean air intake grill with brush
- Remove lint from around device
- Check that mounting is still secure

## 7. Troubleshooting

### Common Problems & Solutions

#### Device Won't Turn On

**Possible causes**:
- [ ] Power switch off → Turn on
- [ ] No electricity → Check circuit breaker
- [ ] Loose connection → Check power cable
- [ ] Blown fuse → Replace fuse (see manual)

---

#### Weak Airflow

**Possible causes**:
- [ ] Filter clogged → Clean or replace filter
- [ ] Intake blocked → Remove obstructions
- [ ] Access door not closed properly → Close firmly

---

#### Loud or Unusual Noise

**Possible causes**:
- [ ] Something touching fan → Turn off, inspect, remove object
- [ ] Loose mounting → Tighten mounting hardware
- [ ] Motor issue → Contact support

⚠️ **If noise continues, turn OFF device and contact support**

---

#### Not Reducing Lint Well

**Possible causes**:
- [ ] Filter needs cleaning → Clean filter
- [ ] Device too small for room → May need additional unit
- [ ] Air leaks around doors/windows → Seal gaps
- [ ] Not running enough hours → Run continuously during work

---

#### Smell or Smoke

**⚠️ STOP IMMEDIATELY**:
1. Turn OFF device
2. Unplug from power
3. Do not restart
4. Contact support or electrician
