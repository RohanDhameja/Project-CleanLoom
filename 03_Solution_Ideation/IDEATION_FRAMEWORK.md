# Solution Ideation Framework

## Purpose

This document guides the brainstorming and concept generation process for the lint control solution. The goal is to generate diverse, creative solutions before converging on the most promising approaches.

---

## Ideation Principles

### 1. Quantity Over Quality (Initially)
- Generate 20+ ideas minimum before evaluating
- No idea is too simple or too complex at this stage
- Build on others' ideas
- Combine different approaches

### 2. Defer Judgment
- Don't critique ideas during brainstorming
- Even "bad" ideas can spark good ones
- Document everything

### 3. Think Divergently
- Explore multiple solution categories
- Consider low-tech to high-tech options
- Think about prevention, capture, and removal

### 4. Stay User-Centered
- Keep workshop constraints in mind
- Remember: cost, simplicity, cultural fit matter
- But don't let constraints kill creativity too early

## Solution Categories to Explore

### Category 1: Air Filtration Systems
**Concept**: Remove lint from air using filters

**Sub-approaches**:
- Box fan + furnace filter (DIY air purifier)
- Ceiling-mounted filtration units
- Multiple small filters throughout room
- Central filtration with ducting
- Wet filtration (water-based capture)

**Examples to explore**:
- Corsi-Rosenthal box design
- HEPA filtration
- Electrostatic filters
- Activated carbon filters
- Multi-stage filtration

---

### Category 2: Ventilation & Air Circulation
**Concept**: Move lint out of workspace or improve air flow

**Sub-approaches**:
- Exhaust fans to remove lint-laden air
- Intake fans with filters for clean air
- Cross-ventilation systems
- Passive ventilation (natural airflow)
- Air curtains to contain lint

**Examples to explore**:
- Window-mounted exhaust fans
- Solar-powered ventilation
- Chimney/stack effect ventilation
- Positive pressure systems
- Localized air circulation

---

### Category 3: Source Capture
**Concept**: Capture lint at the point of generation

**Sub-approaches**:
- Suction attachments for each loom
- Shields or guards around shedding points
- Static-reducing treatments
- Vacuum collection at source
- Enclosures for high-lint areas

**Examples to explore**:
- Shop-vac style attachments
- Flexible ductwork from each loom
- Plexiglass shields with suction
- Magnetic or electrostatic capture
- Lint traps at thread path

---

### Category 4: Preventive Measures
**Concept**: Reduce lint generation at the source

**Sub-approaches**:
- Thread/yarn treatments
- Machine maintenance protocols
- Humidity control (reduce static)
- Thread tension optimization
- Pre-treating cotton to reduce shedding

**Examples to explore**:
- Humidification systems
- Thread conditioning oils
- Regular machine servicing schedules
- Upgrade specific loom components
- Alternative thread sources

---

### Category 5: Protective Equipment
**Concept**: Protect workers from lint exposure

**Sub-approaches**:
- Improved respirator masks
- Eye protection
- Protective clothing
- Personal air filtration devices

**Examples to explore**:
- N95/N99 masks adapted for comfort
- Powered air-purifying respirators (PAPR)
- Lightweight face shields
- Cooling vests with filtration
- Personal wearable air purifiers

---

### Category 6: Cleaning & Maintenance Tools
**Concept**: Make lint removal easier and more effective

**Sub-approaches**:
- Improved cleaning tools
- Scheduled maintenance systems
- Training programs
- Automated cleaning devices

**Examples to explore**:
- Industrial vacuum systems (scaled down)
- Compressed air cleaning stations
- Lint roller tools for machines
- Cleaning protocols and checklists
- Robot cleaners (if feasible)

---

### Category 7: Hybrid Solutions
**Concept**: Combine multiple approaches

**Examples**:
- Filtration + ventilation
- Source capture + room filtration
- Prevention + cleaning tools
- PPE + environmental control

## Ideation Techniques

### Technique 1: Braindumping (Individual)
1. Set timer for 15 minutes
2. Write every idea that comes to mind
3. No filtering or judgment
4. Aim for 30+ ideas
5. Use the prompts below

**Prompts**:
- What if we used ___ technology?
- How do ___ industries handle this?
- What's the simplest possible solution?
- What's the most high-tech solution?
- What if cost was no object?
- What if we had no electricity?
- What if we could only use local materials?

---

### Technique 2: SCAMPER Method

Apply these questions to existing solutions:

- **Substitute**: What materials/components can we substitute?
- **Combine**: What can we combine together?
- **Adapt**: What can we adapt from other industries?
- **Modify**: What can we modify or magnify?
- **Put to other use**: Can we repurpose existing items?
- **Eliminate**: What can we remove or simplify?
- **Reverse/Rearrange**: What if we reversed the approach?

---

### Technique 3: Analogies

**Look at how other industries solve similar problems**:

- How does the mining industry handle dust?
- How do hospitals maintain clean air?
- How do woodworking shops handle sawdust?
- How do bakeries manage flour dust?
- How do stone cutting units manage dust?
- How do automotive paint shops filter air?

---

### Technique 4: Constraint Removal

**Temporarily remove constraints and brainstorm**:

- What if we had unlimited budget?
- What if the workshop could be rebuilt?
- What if we could redesign the looms?
- What if we had industrial-scale equipment?

Then ask: How can we achieve 80% of that result with our actual constraints?

---

### Technique 5: Low-Tech Focus

**Only consider solutions using**:
- Locally available materials
- Basic tools
- Minimal or no electricity
- Traditional construction methods

## Brainstorming Session Plan

### Session 1: Divergent Thinking (No Constraints)
- Duration: 1-2 hours
- Goal: Generate maximum ideas (30+ concepts)
- Method: Individual braindumping + group sharing
- Rule: No criticism, build on each other

### Session 2: Analogies & Research
- Duration: 2-3 hours
- Goal: Learn from other industries
- Method: Research similar problems, bring examples
- Output: 10-15 adapted concepts

### Session 3: Low-Tech Focus
- Duration: 1 hour
- Goal: Ensure affordability
- Method: Constraint-focused brainstorming
- Output: 10+ simple, cheap solutions

### Session 4: Combinations
- Duration: 1 hour
- Goal: Create hybrid solutions
- Method: Mix and match previous ideas
- Output: 5-10 combined approaches
