# Problem Statement: Lint Accumulation in Small Weaving Workshops

## Executive Summary

Small weaving workshops across India face severe lint accumulation issues that affect worker health, productivity, and fabric quality. These workshops operate multiple power looms in enclosed, poorly ventilated spaces, creating hazardous working conditions and economic losses. A low-cost, practical lint control solution is urgently needed.

---

## The Problem in Detail

### Context

- **Target Users**: Small weaving workshop owners and workers
- **Location**: Rural and semi-urban areas across India
- **Scale**: Thousands of workshops, each with 2-10 looms
- **Economic Status**: Low-margin, family-run businesses
- **Current Solution**: None or inadequate (manual cleaning only)

### Root Causes

1. **Enclosed Spaces**: Brick/cement rooms with no windows or ventilation
2. **High-Speed Looms**: Continuous fiber shedding during operation
3. **Multiple Machines**: 2-10 looms operating in tight quarters
4. **No Air Filtration**: No dust extraction or filtration systems
5. **Limited Awareness**: Lack of knowledge about health risks

---

## Problem Evidence

### Visual Evidence

- Heavy lint accumulation on machinery (gears, rods, moving parts)
- Dust visible on ceilings, walls, and beams
- Fibers settling on running fabric during weaving
- Multiple looms operating with minimal spacing
- No visible ventilation paths or air movement

### Documented Impacts

#### 1. Health Impacts
- Persistent coughing and respiratory irritation
- Eye irritation and strain
- Throat and nasal problems
- Long-term risk of respiratory diseases
- Limited access to healthcare or protective equipment

#### 2. Productivity Impacts
- Frequent machine stoppages for cleaning
- Lint causing jams and malfunctions
- Reduced daily output
- Worker discomfort affecting focus and stamina

#### 3. Quality Impacts
- Fibers landing on fabric during weaving
- Additional manual cleaning of finished cloth
- Increased defect rates
- Customer complaints

#### 4. Economic Impacts
- Increased machine maintenance costs
- Faster wear on mechanical parts
- Lost production time
- Reduced fabric value due to defects

#### 5. Environmental Impacts
- Poor working conditions
- Uncomfortable, dusty environment
- Lack of dignity in workspace

## Current Coping Mechanisms

### What People Do Now
1. **Manual Cleaning**: Periodic sweeping and wiping (ineffective)
2. **Fans**: Circulate air but don't remove lint
3. **Opening Doors**: Limited effectiveness, weather-dependent
4. **Masks**: Rarely used, uncomfortable, not effective against fine particles
5. **Nothing**: Most workshops accept it as "part of the job"

### Why Current Solutions Fail
- Don't address root cause (airborne fibers)
- Too expensive (industrial systems)
- Too complex to maintain
- Not designed for small-scale operations
- Require significant space or power

## Problem Constraints

### Technical Constraints
- Limited electrical power (1-2 phase)
- Small room size (10' x 15' to 20' x 30')
- Low ceiling height (8-10 feet)
- No existing ventilation infrastructure
- Cannot interfere with loom operation

### Economic Constraints
- Budget: ₹5,000-15,000 per workshop maximum
- ROI needed within 1-2 years
- Cannot require ongoing high operating costs
- Must use locally available materials when possible

### Social Constraints
- Low literacy levels (visual instructions needed)
- Traditional practices (resistance to change)
- Limited technical knowledge
- Cannot disrupt daily production
- Must be culturally acceptable
