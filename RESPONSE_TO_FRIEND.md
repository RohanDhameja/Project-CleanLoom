## Answer 2: Nature of Solution

### **Hybrid Approach: Technical + Behavioral + Social**

**Primary: Technical Solution (70%)**
- A physical device/system to remove lint from air
- Options: Air filtration, ventilation, source capture, or combination
- Must be: Affordable (<₹10,000), low power, simple to maintain

**Supporting: Behavioral Component (20%)**
- Maintenance protocols and usage guidelines
- Regular cleaning schedules
- Simple, visual instructions for users

**Enabling: Social Component (10%)**
- Community adoption and peer learning
- Success stories and word-of-mouth
- Group purchasing for better prices

**Why Hybrid?**
- Technical solution addresses the root cause (airborne lint)
- Behavioral ensures it's used correctly and maintained
- Social enables adoption at scale through trust and community

**Most Likely:** Low-cost air filtration/ventilation system with simple maintenance protocols, adopted through community networks.

## Answer 3: Pictorial Evidence Collection

### Strategy:

**1. Permission First**
- Contact workshop owners in advance
- Explain project purpose
- Get verbal/written consent
- Offer to share photos with them
- Respect privacy (blur faces if requested)

**2. Systematic Documentation**
- Use smartphone cameras (accessible, good quality)
- Follow photo checklist:
  - Workshop overview (wide shots)
  - Lint evidence (close-ups on machines, ceiling, walls)
  - Infrastructure (ventilation, electrical, dimensions)
  - Workers (with permission)
  - Existing solutions they've tried

**3. Organization**
- File naming: `YYYYMMDD_Location_Subject_###.jpg`
- Store in: GitHub repository (`08_Assets/Photos/Workshop_Photos/`)
- Create photo log with descriptions
- Backup to cloud storage

**4. Privacy & Ethics**
- Always ask before photographing people
- Blur faces if requested
- Don't share identifiable info publicly without permission
- Keep contact information private

**Timeline:**
- Weeks 1-2: Collect 50-100 photos from 5-10 workshops
- Week 3: Organize and catalog
- Week 4: Fill gaps if needed
- Ongoing: Continue during testing (before/after comparisons)

**Goal:** Build comprehensive visual evidence library that shows:
- The problem clearly
- Why existing solutions don't work
- Impact of our solution (later)
